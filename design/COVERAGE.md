- **Per-screen interaction / TalkBack annotation layers** — not drawn as overlays; drag alternatives are visible on screen (Schedule… on O8/T2/T8, ± estimate buttons in T2).
- **Global loading/error states** are drawn where they apply (M1, M2 loading; T9, X3, S10 errors); screens that only read local data have no separate loading frame.
